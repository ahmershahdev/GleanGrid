# Customer Guide

## Create an account

1. **Join GleanGrid** → choose *Customer* and enter your name, username, e-mail, phone and address (all required by the SRS).
2. Tick the reCAPTCHA box and submit.
3. Enter the **six-digit code** we e-mail you. You can browse without verifying, but ordering needs a verified address.

## Find food

- **Markets** — a live map of every market with trading days and hours; filter by day, see *open today*, or sort by distance with *Near me*.
- **Farmers** — stall profiles with ratings, operating days, markets and this week's stock. Sort by top rated, A–Z or newest.
- **Produce** — search and filter by category, market, day, price and *in stock*; sort by featured, price, rating, popularity or newest.
- **Search anywhere** — press `Ctrl K` / `⌘K` or `/` for instant results across produce, farmers and markets, with typo suggestions.
- **Basket Buddy** — the assistant at the bottom of every page answers questions about timings, availability, pickup and products.

## Pre-order

1. Add items to your basket (the basket icon bumps as items fly in).
2. Open the basket → **Checkout**. Each farmer becomes its own pre-order.
3. Pick a **pickup date and window** for each; full windows are hidden.
4. Add a coupon if you have one — the discount is shown before you confirm.
5. Confirm. Nothing is charged; you'll get a confirmation e-mail with your **order code**.

## Manage orders

**My orders** lists everything with its status: *placed → accepted → ready → completed*.

- **Modify** or **Cancel** until the farmer's cut-off (shown on the order).
- **Reorder** any past order in one tap.
- The order page has the pickup time, market, a **map with directions**, and buttons to **call, WhatsApp or e-mail the farmer**.

## At the market

Show your order code, check your produce, pay the farmer (cash, bank transfer or mobile wallet). You only pay for what you accept. See the [Returns policy](https://gleangrid.ahmershah.dev/returns) for problems found at home.

## Favourites, alerts and family

- Tap ♥ on any product, stall or market — it flies into the header heart instantly. Tap again to remove it and watch it fly back out.
- Turn on **restock alerts** on favourites to be told when a sold-out item returns.
- **Family sharing** links household accounts so members can see and collect each other's orders.

## Reviews

After an order is completed, rate the farmer and each product (1–5 ★ with a comment). Reviews appear on stall and product pages; farmers can reply.

## Account & security

Profile → change details, language and password; see signed-in devices and **sign out other devices**. You'll get an e-mail whenever a new device signs in.
