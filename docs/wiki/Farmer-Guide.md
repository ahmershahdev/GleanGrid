# Farmer Guide

## Register your stall

**Join GleanGrid → Farmer** and provide the stall/business name, contact person, phone, e-mail and address. Verify your e-mail with the six-digit code. Your stall is then **pending** until an administrator approves it (usually within a day) — you'll get an e-mail when it's live.

While pending you can already complete your **Stall profile**.

## Stall profile (`/farmer/stall-profile`)

- Logo, cover, tagline and story.
- **Markets you sell at**, with your stall number at each.
- Operating days.
- Location: address plus a **map pin** (drag it) — latitude and longitude are stored for directions.

The dashboard shows a setup checklist (profile, markets, pickup windows, products) until everything is done.

## Products (`/farmer/products`)

- Add, edit, view and delete products with name, category, price, unit, quantity available, description and image (photos are compressed to WebP in your browser before upload).
- **Weekly stock template:** set each product's usual weekly quantity, then press **Apply weekly template** at the start of the week to reset all stock in one go.
- Status per product: **available**, **sold out**, or **temporarily unavailable**. Customers who favourited an item are alerted automatically when it comes back.

## Pickup windows & cut-off (`/farmer/pickup-windows`)

- Add a window per market day with start/end time and **capacity** (maximum orders). Capacity is enforced atomically — a full window disappears from checkout.
- Set one **order cut-off** (1–168 hours before a window). Customers can change or cancel until then; after it, orders lock so you can pick and pack.

## Orders (`/farmer/orders`)

1. New pre-orders arrive as **placed** (you get an e-mail + in-app alert).
2. **Accept** or **Decline** (with an optional note). Declining releases stock and any coupon.
3. On market day mark **Ready** — the customer is notified.
4. When they collect and pay, mark **Completed**.

Each order shows a packing list, customer name and phone, pickup window and market.

## Insights (dashboard)

Total, pending, to-prepare and ready orders; lifetime and this-week revenue; open pipeline value; an 8-week revenue chart; **best-selling products**; upcoming pickups; low-stock warnings.

## Reviews & coupons

- **Reviews:** read what customers say and post a public reply.
- **Coupons:** create percentage or fixed codes for your stall with dates, minimum basket, maximum discount and usage limits.

## Tips

- Keep stock honest — overselling is impossible, but underselling leaves money at home.
- Mark items sold out instead of declining orders.
- Reply to reviews; it builds trust with new customers.
